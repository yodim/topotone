Topotone

Sound turned into a landscape. The melody raises the mountains, beats plant trees and the bass carves rivers. With no sound at all, the land follows a quiet music of its own.

Press Song to play a song from this device, or Mic to let the land listen to the room. Style draws the same world another way, New world makes a different one, and Export saves the whole stretch as one long picture.

You can also drop a song anywhere on the page. The keys:

Your audio never leaves this device. There are no network calls, no analytics and no uploads.
